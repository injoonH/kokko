# Ranked seasons

[v2.2.0](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/releases/tag/v2.2.0) adds seasonal LP in [issue #22](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/22). Kokko displays lifetime Glicko Elo for skill, probabilities and chart history. Seasonal rank measures the climb through confirmed games. Read [the Glicko contract](RATINGS.md) and [the confirmation contract](PUBLIC.md) for the sources of those results.

The ladder borrows placements, divisions and competitive apex slots from [Riot's ranked guide](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues), [LP and MMR](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/mmr-rank-and-lp), [placements](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/placements-promotions-series-demotions-and-decay) and [apex tiers](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/master-grandmaster-and-challenger-the-apex-tiers). The formulas, school-sized slots and [original badminton crests](CRESTS.md) are Kokko choices.

## Start and carryover

- Singles and doubles run independent placements, ratings and leaderboard places. A ranked year begins on the configured Korean date and rolls over on its anniversary. Accepted games qualify by **played date** and canonical within-day order, so late approvals count on the date played.
- At first startup the server records the next Korean date as the opening. For an isolated copy, an optional `KOKKO_RANKED_START=YYYY-MM-DD` sets the initial date, including a historical date for rehearsal. The date and rules version persist. A conflicting environment value stops startup. Leave the variable unset for the normal rollout.
- Earlier games still supply Elo, confidence and history. A player has five placement matches whenever entering a new year. A provisional win adds LP up to the placement cap. A provisional loss costs zero LP and still affects Glicko. MVP placements start no higher than Platinum IV 0 LP in year one or Diamond III 0 LP in later years. Provisional players appear below placed players without an ordinal position.
- The fifth confirmed game establishes the tier and starts that year's peak. Progress starts again after the annual boundary. The previous year's established result contributes to the new placement seed if the player participated in the immediately preceding year.

## Rule version 1

Let `E` and `RD` be the player's before-game Glicko and aged deviation. Let `p` be the existing team win probability and `s` be 1 for a win or 0 for a loss.

1. The target points are `4 × (E − 700)`. Placement begins from the target minus 400 and minus `3 × max(0, RD − 80)`. When the prior year's rank is established, average that seed with the final prior total minus 400. Round the seed down to a multiple of 100 and clamp it from zero to the placement cap.
2. After seeding, point change is `round(50 × (s − p) + alignment)`. Alignment is the rank target minus current total, divided by 100 and weighted by confidence `clamp((350 − RD)/270, 0, 1)`, then clamped to ±8 and rounded. Wins earn 1 to 40 and losses cost 1 to 40 before the placement, demotion and zero floor rules. At even odds with alignment zero, the nominal change is ±25 LP.
3. Each division takes 100 LP. Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald and Diamond each have IV through I. Promotions carry overflow. A promotion into a higher tier or completing placements above Iron grants one loss of protection when crossing that tier floor. It leaves the player at 0 LP in that tier. The shield is then spent until the next tier promotion.
4. Master begins at 2,800 total points and displays LP above that floor. Starting at the year-one placement cap of 1,600 leaves 1,200 points to Master. Two Grandmaster slots open at 400+ Master LP and one Challenger slot at 800+ Master LP in each pool. Assign Challenger first. Slots can stay empty. Sort applicants by total LP, unrounded Glicko at the close, then stable player ID for an exact tie. Same tier and points share a public ordinal.
5. Apply the previous completed Korean date's slot assignment. Today's confirmed games move LP immediately and move competitive seats after midnight. Backdated approvals, corrections and withdrawals rebuild the affected history, including peaks and seat assignments. Suspended accounts leave the current standings and seat calculation.

## Evidence and operation

A read-only memory copy of 34 singles and 67 doubles produced no Master rank under either a cold start or a later seeded start. The strongest warm-start result was Platinum IV. Synthetic, fixed-skill schedules at the placement cap took 67 matches with 70% wins and 55 matches with 80% wins against balanced opposition. A player at 1,325 Elo winning 90% of games when already 90% favored did not reach Master in 200 games. These are pace checks on a small pool, not a prediction of ranks or a rating calibration result. [Replay note](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/22#issuecomment-7080608545)

The server writes Elo, LP and ranked history inside the same accepted-result transaction. `kokko_rank_entries`, `kokko_rank_games`, and `kokko_rank_days` are projections of confirmed match history. Startup can regenerate them, including after time on the previous image. The Elo view continues to read the existing Glicko cache. Programmatic consumers of `/api/kokko/arena/leaderboard` keep Elo order by default. Use `view=ranked` for the active year, its placements and LP places. The browser defaults to that seasonal view and offers **Elo** beside it. Both share the existing friends filter and 25-row pages.

A code-only rollback to v2.1.0 keeps the additive tables and new matches. The next v2.2+ start rebuilds LP from accepted history. For restoring an archive, use the matching image and the recovery procedure in the README. Changes to the pace or calendar require a new documented rules version, not an edit to the current tables.
