# November 22, 2026

The owner asked how to configure Kakao Login on the Pi, requested a square icon based on the Kokko mascot, and configured optional nickname consent. The owner confirmed a real Kakao callback worked and reported that signing in directly had started a separate signup. The Pi still had public signup disabled, and a read-only check verified the unfinished signup had no profile or membership.

[v2.0.1](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/releases/tag/v2.0.1) lets an existing member sign in with the original provider and complete **설정 → 로그인 계정 연결** to recover a second provider from an unfinished signup. The link requires proof of both accounts through the usual sign-in and linking flows. It discards the unused signup only after moving the verified login and preserves the original account and game data. Active and suspended accounts cannot be combined through this flow.

[Issue #14](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/14) tracks the remaining live provider checks and the deployment receipt. The successful Kakao callback does not verify open registration, which remains gated while [#16](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/16) is in progress.

The owner requested a closer League of Legends ranking system after observing that 1,300 Elo was too easy to reach, and asked for a Global Leaderboard tab. They approved the revised plan. [v2.1.0](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/releases/tag/v2.1.0) implements the public **전체 순위** tab with today's Elo ordering and moves Settings to the signed-in header menu. [Issue #23](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/23) tracks this independent release. [Issue #22](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/22) records the next phase with separate seasonal LP, placements, divisions and competitive apex slots. Candidate thresholds are subject to replay and calibration before the ranked season opens.

[v2.2.0](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/releases/tag/v2.2.0) ships the LP phase. The selected formula was replayed on an isolated copy of 101 matches and checked with synthetic balanced and heavily favored schedules. The first year opens on the next Korean date after startup, with old results carried into lifetime Elo and placement seeding. Profiles now show current and peak season rank, and global standings offer both Season Rank and Elo. [Rules and calibration](../RANKED.md)
