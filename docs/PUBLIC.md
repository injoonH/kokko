# Players, friends and confirmed results

The owner changed the public-service direction on 2026-11-21: replace the group product with global standings, player profiles, mutual friends and a temporary quick-match roster. Any active registered players can play together; friendship is a selection shortcut. Existing match details are public in v2.0.0. New ranked results require confirmation by one opposing participant. This supersedes self-service groups in the earlier assessment and decisions 16/19, while retaining their global rating and account-ownership invariants.

## Implementation contract

- Public projections contain nicknames, avatars, unique player handles, match dates/lineups/scores and accepted rating history. Never expose provider details, email, account privileges, invitations, request payloads, moderation reports or raw audit snapshots. Deleted results remain excluded from public history. Retain suspended players in historical records but exclude them from current standings/new lineups.
- Keep existing accounts, authorship, matches, canonical order, audits, retries and rating caches. Existing results are accepted history and need no retroactive confirmation. Group tables/IDs can remain for recovery and old drafts, with no membership requirement in the public product.
- New submissions are pending proposals. The submitter must participate; the confirmer must be an active player on the opposite team. A teammate, the submitter, an unrelated player or an account administrator cannot bypass this rule. Friendships are not required.
- A pending correction/withdrawal leaves the previous accepted match and ratings intact. Approval checks the accepted version, applies the proposal once and replays affected global disciplines atomically. Rejection/cancellation has no rating effect. Correcting scores, dates or order needs fresh approval; ordinary corrections retain the accepted lineup so a different opponent cannot erase another player's result. Administrators can void invalid results with an audited reason, closing pending requests. Reopening after review only permits a new opponent-confirmed restoration; it does not restore ratings.
- Reserve canonical playing order when a proposal is submitted, so confirming later games first does not reverse their eventual rating timeline. Explicit historical placement uses accepted-match anchors. Concurrent changes and retries must not duplicate results or silently overwrite newer versions.
- Friends use requests/acceptance, removal and blocking. Blocking removes the relationship and prevents new requests/lineups involving the pair. It never rewrites accepted history. Reports are private to the reporter and service administrators and require an explicit moderation action.
- Public reads and player search are bounded/paginated (history/inbox/standings/social 25, search 20; chart 200 plus baseline, pairs top 50, day selection 1,000, quick directory 100, court attendance 12). Exact social-target lookup handles searched players outside the current page. Profiles provide exact aggregates/activity independently of history pagination. New users start unranked at 1,000 / RD 350; established current/history ratings stay unchanged by publication. Show RD and explain that disconnected playing pools do not establish comparable absolute skill.
- Match entry keeps scores, same-player/rotated next games, prediction, simulation, attendance and unresolved-save protections. Friends/recent players are quick choices; search can select any active unblocked player. Resolve pre-upgrade ambiguous requests through their original retry record before converting a draft into a new proposal.

## Rollout

v2.0.0 ([PR #20](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/pull/20)) implements this contract under [issue #15](https://github.com/injoonH/kokko/issues/15); its implementing PR records validation and the issue records actual deployment/recovery evidence. Keep `KOKKO_PUBLIC_SIGNUP=false` until #14 actual provider callbacks and #16 launch checks are complete. Public match/profile visibility is authorized; it does not grant permission to disclose identity-provider data or silently enable registration. Use a pre-upgrade backup and published-image restoration/migration rehearsal before deployment. Never downgrade only the image after public-model writes.

Validation for PR #20: `pnpm check`, four release-tool tests, 51 frontend tests, Go race tests, `pnpm build`, and 19 mobile browser tests passed locally. Mobile/desktop screenshots were inspected. Populated migration and actual archive restoration preserve source/caches; separate tests cover late/concurrent approval, moderation, exact aggregates beyond chart/history bounds, draft retries and public field projection. Actual provider callbacks and workload capacity remain unverified launch gates.
